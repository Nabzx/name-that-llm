# Video script: three minutes

One speaker, one screen. Present `documents/presentation.pdf` (or `presentation.html` full screen) and read `documents/speaker-notes.md`, which holds the spoken words for each slide. About 400 words, three minutes.

## Recording

1. Open the deck full screen and the notes on a second screen or a printout.
2. Record one clip per slide with QuickTime or OBS, then join them. A fumbled line costs one clip, not the whole take.
3. Upload unlisted to YouTube or Drive and put the link in the hand-in form.
